# Research & verification — how evidence is handled

> **Status:** `DOCUMENTED` and in active use. This page describes the protocol and names the research lines by theme; findings, references and primary records are internal.

## The three-layer protocol

Every line of external research lives in three layers with different edit rules:

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Primary record** | **Immutable.** The original result is archived verbatim and never edited — a primary record that gets "corrected" stops being evidence |
| **Line report** | Versioned, with a changelog. Corrections happen by new version, never by silent deletion |
| **Living integrator** | Holds only what emerges *across* lines, with declared triggers for revision |

## The method rules

A small set of non-negotiable rules travels with every piece of evidence:

- **Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.** "I did not find X" is never written as "X does not exist."
- **Reservations travel with the datum.** A number never sheds its caveat on the way to a summary.
- **Empty fronts are declared, not filled.** If a search found nothing, the report says so instead of padding.
- **Genre-appropriate rigour, declared:** structured appraisal for grey literature, rapid-review discipline with enumerated deviations for scientific lines, and a source hierarchy separating applicable law from non-binding signal for normative lines.
- **Single-reviewer limitations are stated**, not laundered.

## The research lines, by theme

The external research series spans, among others: **the competitive landscape of emotional-support apps** · **recent scientific evidence on conversational support agents**, organised in seven fronts · **the legal framework for minors** in conversational agents · **internationalisation standards** · **jurisdiction, international data transfers and legal language** in Colombia · **the Spanish-language market** · **localisation quality and psychometric adaptation** · **bilingual LLM parity**. Together they hold roughly 250 references.

Alongside them run the programme's scientific lines: the study of meditation's effects with psychophysiological measurement (introduced in the [main README](../../README.md)), and an international research collaboration around a mindfulness and emotional-intelligence programme — credited in the README's acknowledgements; its scope will be described when that line is documented here.

## Verification passes

Published claims are periodically **re-checked against primary sources** in dedicated verification passes. When a check finds that a claim overstated its source, the claim is corrected by version — with the correction recorded, not overwritten. Legal texts are verified against the literal wording of the instrument, not against secondary commentary; where only secondary sources exist, the claim is downgraded accordingly.

## Why publish this and not the findings

The findings feed decisions and product work that are still in progress; publishing them now would hand over conclusions without their context or their caveats. The protocol, by contrast, is the part worth showing: it is what makes any individual finding trustworthy — and it is verifiable in the way this documentation itself is written.
